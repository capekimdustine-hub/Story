Black Clover

Author: Yūki Tabata

Genre: Fantasy / Action

Synopsis

In a world where everyone is born with magic, Asta is the only one without any. While his rival Yuno possesses incredible talent and a legendary Grimoire, Asta receives a Five-Leaf Clover Grimoire that grants him the power to nullify all magic. With unwavering determination, he sets out to become the Wizard King.

Chapter 1: Asta and Yuno

Long ago, it was said that anyone who possessed great magic would rise to become the Wizard King — the strongest mage in the Clover Kingdom.

In a small orphanage in the village of Hage, two boys were left on the same day they were born. One was named Yuno — quiet, graceful, and blessed with immense magical power. The other was named Asta — loud, energetic, and completely unable to use even a single spell.

While the other children practiced magic, Asta trained his body relentlessly. Every day, rain or shine, he ran, lifted boulders, and pushed himself beyond his limits. People laughed and told him it was impossible — that without magic, he could never be anything important.

But Asta never gave up. "I'm going to become the Wizard King!" he would shout, no matter how many times they mocked him.

— End of Chapter 1 —

Naruto

Author: Masashi Kishimoto

Genre: Adventure / Action

Synopsis

Naruto Uzumaki is a young ninja who dreams of becoming the Hokage — the leader of the village — so people will finally acknowledge him. But he carries a dark secret: the Nine-Tailed Fox demon is sealed inside him, making him an outcast.

Chapter 1: Naruto Uzumaki!

In the Hidden Leaf Village, there lived a boy named Naruto Uzumaki. The villagers hated and feared him because the Nine-Tailed Fox that once attacked their home was sealed within his body.

Alone and lonely, Naruto pulled pranks and caused trouble just to get attention. While other kids dreamed of being ninja, Naruto was determined to become the greatest ninja of all — the Hokage.

One day, his teacher Iruka Umino gave Naruto the first real compliment he had ever received. When Mizuki tricked Naruto into stealing a forbidden scroll, it was Iruka who stood between Naruto and danger, proving that someone truly cared for him.

— End of Chapter 1 —

One Piece

Author: Eiichiro Oda

Genre: Adventure / Fantasy

Synopsis

Years ago, the legendary Pirate King Gol D. Roger revealed that his treasure, the One Piece, was hidden at the end of the Grand Line. Now, countless pirates sail the seas in search of it. Monkey D. Luffy dreams of finding the ultimate treasure and becoming the King of the Pirates.

Chapter 1: Romance Dawn

In a small village by the sea, a young boy named Luffy dreamed of the ocean. Inspired by the pirate Shanks, who once saved his life, Luffy decided that one day he would sail across the Grand Line, find the legendary treasure One Piece, and become the King of the Pirates.

When Luffy accidentally ate the Gum-Gum Fruit, his body turned into rubber — but he could never swim again. That didn't stop him. He trained every day, knowing that one day he would set sail.

— End of Chapter 1 —

Attack on Titan

Author: Hajime Isayama

Genre: Dark Fantasy / Drama

Synopsis

Humanity lives inside cities surrounded by enormous walls, terrified of the Titans — giant humanoid creatures that devour people seemingly for no reason. But when the outer wall is breached, a young boy named Eren Yeager vows to kill every Titan on Earth and set humanity free.

Chapter 1: The Fall of Shiganshina

For a hundred years, humanity had lived in peace behind three mighty walls. But that peace was shattered when a Colossal Titan taller than Wall Maria appeared out of nowhere and kicked a hole through the gate.

As smaller Titans flooded into the district of Shiganshina, chaos and terror spread. Eren Yeager watched in horror as a Titan swallowed his mother right before his eyes. With nowhere else to go, Eren, his adopted sister Mikasa, and their friend Armin fled to safety inside the inner walls.

— End of Chapter 1 —

Demon Slayer

Author: Koyoharu Gotouge

Genre: Fantasy / Action

Synopsis

Tanjiro Kamado is a kind-hearted boy who lived peacefully in the mountains with his family. One day, he returns home to find his entire family slaughtered by demons — and his younger sister Nezuko turned into one. Determined to avenge his family and cure his sister, Tanjiro becomes a Demon Slayer.

Chapter 1: Cruelty

Tanjiro Kamado lived high in the mountains with his mother and five younger siblings. Their home was simple but filled with warmth. One winter morning, Tanjiro went down the mountain to sell charcoal in the village below.

When he returned at night, something was wrong. Snow covered the ground — and so was blood. His entire family had been killed by a demon. Miraculously, his sister Nezuko was still alive, but her eyes had changed. She had become a demon too.

Even in her transformed state, Nezuko protected her brother from harm. Deep in the snow, Tanjiro made a promise: he would find a way to turn Nezuko back into a human and defeat the demon responsible for destroying their family.

— End of Chapter 1 —
